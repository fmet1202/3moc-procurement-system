One Name, One Vision, Complete Procurement Reliability

When three founding partners who all bear the name Abraham joined forces in Addis Ababa, they didn't just form a partnership—they established a standard.

In finance and international commerce, "Triple-A" signifies the highest grade of creditworthiness, dependability, and performance. 3 MOC brings that exact standard to Ethiopian public procurement.

Government ministries and public agencies often struggle with fragmented procurement: stationery purchased from one broker, sanitation chemicals from another, and cafeteria kitchenware from a third. Deliveries are frequently late, samples don't match delivered goods, and multiple invoices complicate accounting.

3 MOC solves this completely. We bid on consolidated lots, provide a single accountable proforma, enforce strict physical quality control, and deliver everything directly to your stores.
