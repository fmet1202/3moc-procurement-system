Three founding partners, all named Abraham, formed 3 MOC to fix a recurring problem in Ethiopian public procurement: fragmented orders.

Government offices typically buy stationery from one broker, cleaning supplies from another, and furniture from a third — with mismatched samples, late deliveries, and separate invoices to reconcile.

3 MOC bids on the full lot instead. One proforma, one quality check against the samples we quoted, one delivery to your store.

Registration

  • Entity: General supply partnership
  • Based in: Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
  • Platform: e-GP compliant supplier
  • Tax: TIN registered, 15% VAT

Who does what

  • Sourcing: wholesale pricing and quality control
  • Compliance: tender specs and e-GP paperwork
  • Logistics: packaging and store delivery
